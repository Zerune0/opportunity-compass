# Frozen product scope
Authority: the user's 15-page final integrated product specification, confirmed 2026-11-17. Everything here is planned.

## Core journey
Real opportunity → official eligibility → gap and reachability analysis → selectable growth routes → real evidence → adaptive reassessment → application or participation.

## Full scope retained
- Verified discovery: research, scholarships, hackathons, internships, fellowships, public-service and practical projects.
- Explainable eligibility: satisfied, not satisfied and unknown per requirement; separate mandatory rules, preferences and suggested skills.
- Gap analysis: knowledge, experience, materials, time, cost, immutable restrictions and missing information.
- Cross-opportunity planning and material reuse, with opportunity-specific editing costs.
- Adaptive Path Optimizer: reverse planning, comparable routes, dependencies, deadlines, budget, resource use and recovery after blocked goals.
- Skill Passport, evidence status, recommendation replay and voluntary outcome tracking.
- Opportunity World: Quest Board, Quest Details, Adventure Map, Inventory and Quest Journal; accessible Classic View.
- Connect: trusted Quest Creator, optional Talent Board and explainable invitations with consent, refusal and blocking.
- Organizer support: blind spots, aggregated barrier reports, what-if interventions and measured outcomes.
- Shared decision mechanism: interventions x, resource allocations y and route reachability z.
- Fairness audit, independent verifier and comparisons against strong baselines.

## Shared constraints
Account for fixed, marginal and verified shared costs; count fixed costs once. Enforce capacity per time slot, mutual exclusion, prerequisites, deadlines and approvals. Pending support creates conditional advice only. Official hard restrictions cannot be changed to manufacture feasibility.

At a common evaluation time and assumptions, let R0 be original coverage and R1 globally allocated coverage. Report gains |R1 - R0|, losses |R0 - R1| and net gains minus losses. Default protection requires zero losses. Tradeoff mode requires an explicit loss cap. Fairness thresholds use eligible denominators, handle zero denominators and declare overlapping-group handling. Report infeasibility without silently relaxing constraints.

## Non-goals
Large recruitment or social platforms, AI interviewers, virtual currency, public ability rankings, complete course platforms and opportunity access locked by game progress.
