# Privacy and evidence
Planned requirements, not implemented protections. (Current build: no accounts or backend; student data stays in the browser's localStorage.)

Private Skill Passports by default. Talent Board sharing must be voluntary, field-specific and revocable. Do not reveal contact details, precise location, private application materials or sensitive information by default. Organizer reports require authorized, suitably aggregated data and adequate samples.

Distinguish Self-reported, Evidence attached, Reviewed and Unverified. A link is not professional certification. Recommendations explain used facts, rules and changes, rather than exposing private model reasoning.

Label user-created tasks separately from officially verified opportunities; label synthetic users and fixtures explicitly. Plan publisher checks, limits, reporting, moderation and stricter defaults for minors.

Conditional funding is not awarded funding. Feasible preparation is not official eligibility or admission probability. Simulated coverage is not measured causal impact.
