# Demo storyboard - phase 3

Target: approximately 3 minutes 30 seconds. Devpost currently recommends 2-5 minutes, rather than imposing a mandatory length. Source checked 2026-11-19: https://sq-hacks.devpost.com/rules and https://sq-hacks.devpost.com/ . Recheck immediately before recording and verify the accepted video hosting/link format in the submission form; do not assume a platform.

## Recording gates

- Record the final deployed main at https://opportunity-compass-six.vercel.app, using a clean browser profile and a fixed window size. This document is a rehearsal plan, not evidence that every scene works online.
- Team lead must confirm the demo opportunity ID, second opportunity ID, student sample and organizer scenario. Do not invent missing fees, weekly hours or deadlines to force a reachable result.
- Confirm the organizer-to-student support bridge is deployed and the same profile/opportunity is used throughout. All support remains Simulated; students remain Sample.
- Keep official sources and unknown fields visible. Within reach is not admission. A backup is not sync. Do not describe local Connect demos as real communication.
- Do not enable notifications or expose personal tabs. Ask the user to enable their preferred quiet recording setup; do not silently change system settings.
- Capture a clean run before narration. Cuts may remove waiting, but must not imply a resource bridge exists when it does not.

## Honest fallback for the final scene

Use only after the team lead confirms the bridge is not available in the final deployment:

> This organizer scenario is simulated. It shows how support could change reachability in the model; writing that support back into the student journey is not connected in this version.

If the scenario uses a Sample opportunity instead of a verified real opportunity, explicitly say:

> This final support scenario uses a clearly labelled sample opportunity, because we could not verify all the information needed for a real case.

Do not show an edited before/after student screen to imply a missing bridge works.
